Weekly prep rhythm

Sunday

First prep • 60–90 min

Prepare Monday through Wednesday breakfasts and snacks. Cook grains and sauces, then poultry and beef meals. Cool promptly and refrigerate in labeled single-serving containers.

Wednesday

Midweek prep • 45–75 min

Prepare Thursday through Sunday breakfasts and snacks. Cook remaining grains, pasta, and vegetables. Cook seafood close to the day you will eat it.

Each evening

Tomorrow check • 5 min

Move frozen food to the refrigerator, pack the next day’s meals, and add fresh greens, herbs, yogurt sauces, or crunchy toppings only after reheating.

Mild heat only. Heat-forward recipes and strong hot sauces are excluded. A small amount of optional chilli flakes is okay—use lightly or leave it out.
